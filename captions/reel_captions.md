## Reel 1 — "$252K from a fee" (output/short01_preview.mp4)
Same job. Same salary. Same savings. Yet one person retires with $252,626 less.

The reason is a fee you never see on your paycheck: the expense ratio on your 401(k) funds. At 0.10% vs 1.00%, a $100K balance plus $500 a month over 30 years ends at about $1.31M vs $1.06M.

Check yours in 2 minutes: log in, open your fund list, find "expense ratio." Under 0.20% is great.

## Reel 2 — "What is an expense ratio?"
Your 401(k) charges you every year, and you never get a bill.

It's called the expense ratio: a small percentage taken straight out of your balance. 0.10% sounds like nothing. Over 30 years, it adds up to tens of thousands of dollars.

Save this, then check your plan's funds today. 📌
