### The test protocol (≈30 min)

1. **Pick a real graded shot** from a delivered client project. Something you
   already colour-graded yourself, where you know every decision you made and why.
2. **Run the ungraded original through Pika's Color Grade.**
3. **Put them side by side.** Yours vs. its.
4. **Write down, before you shoot:**
   - What did it get right that surprised you?
   - Where exactly did it break? (skin tones? highlight rolloff? consistency across a cut?)
   - Would this have passed your client — yes or no?
5. **Optional second pass:** run the same clip through Relight Media. Relighting
   is the harder problem; it's where the honest failure usually shows up.

**If it turns out good — say so.** A creative admitting a tool beat them is far
more interesting than another "AI still can't do it" take. Let the result decide
the Reel, not the other way round.
