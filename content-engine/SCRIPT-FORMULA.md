# The Daily Reel Script Formula

Target: **38-45 seconds.** That's ~110-130 spoken words. Longer than 60s and retention collapses.

## Structure (five beats, timed)

| Beat | Seconds | Words | Job |
|------|---------|-------|-----|
| **1. Hook** | 0-3 | 8-12 | Stop the scroll. Never start with "Hey guys". |
| **2. The News** | 3-12 | 25-30 | What happened, in plain language. One sentence. |
| **3. Why It Matters** | 12-25 | 35-40 | The consequence. Who wins, who loses. This is your value. |
| **4. The Actionable** | 25-38 | 30-35 | What the viewer does tomorrow. A tool, a prompt, a workflow. |
| **5. CTA** | 38-45 | 10-15 | One ask only. Comment-bait > "follow me". |

## Hook formulas that work in this niche (rotate, never repeat within a week)

1. **The number** — "OpenAI just made 400 million people's jobs easier. Here's how."
2. **The contradiction** — "Everyone's wrong about what [X] launched yesterday."
3. **The direct address** — "If you're a designer, today was a bad day. Let me explain."
4. **The free thing** — "This costs 200 dollars a month. It just became free."
5. **The timeline** — "Six months ago this was impossible. Watch."

## Rules
- **One idea per Reel.** Two ideas = zero ideas.
- **Say the tool name out loud AND put it on screen.** Saves are driven by "I need to remember this."
- **No jargon without a 3-word translation.** "Multimodal — meaning it sees images."
- **End on a question, not a plea.** "Would you let it write your code? Tell me." beats "Follow for more."
- **Caption ≠ script.** Caption carries the links and the long context. Script carries the energy.

## Caption template
```
[One-line restatement of the hook]

Here's the breakdown:
→ [point 1]
→ [point 2]
→ [point 3]

[The question from your CTA]

Source: [publication name]

#AI #ArtificialIntelligence #TechNews #AITools #[tool name] #ContentCreator #CreativeAI #FutureOfWork
```
Keep hashtags at 8-12. Mix: 3 broad, 5 niche, 2 specific-to-the-story.

## Posting
- **Best window (India):** 8-10 PM IST weekdays. Second-best: 7-9 AM.
- **Daily is right**, but daily-mediocre loses to 5x-strong. If a day has no real story, post a tool breakdown or a "here's my workflow" instead — never post filler news.
- **First 30 minutes decide everything.** Reply to every comment in that window.
