# Worked Example — what a finished daily script looks like

> This is a **format demo** using a generic story. Real scripts get built from that
> day's actual Tier-1 headlines. Never publish a script without opening the source first.

---

**REEL — [Date] — "The free tier changed everything"**
**Story source:** [publication + link]
**Runtime target:** 42s

---

### 🎬 HOOK (0-3s)
> *Talking head, no intro, start mid-energy.*

**"This tool cost two hundred dollars a month. As of this morning, it's free."**

`ON SCREEN: FREE. (big, centered, 0.5s after you say it)`

---

### 📰 THE NEWS (3-12s)

**"[Company] just dropped [feature] into their free tier. No credit card, no waitlist. The same thing agencies were charging clients for last week."**

`ON SCREEN: product screenshot / screen recording of the actual thing`
`B-ROLL: your own screen, signing up in real time — authenticity beats stock footage`

---

### 💡 WHY IT MATTERS (12-25s)

**"Here's what nobody's saying. This doesn't just save you money — it deletes an entire freelance category. The people who sold this as a service have about ninety days to find a new offer. And the people who learn to use it well? They just got a superpower for free."**

`ON SCREEN: split text — "SELLING IT ❌ | USING IT ✅"`

---

### 🛠 THE ACTIONABLE (25-38s)

**"Do this tonight. Sign up, and run one job you'd normally outsource. Just one. Time it. Then compare that to what you paid last month. That number is your new margin."**

`ON SCREEN: 3-step list appearing one line at a time`

---

### 🎯 CTA (38-45s)

**"Is this the best thing that happened to creators this year — or the scariest? I genuinely can't decide. Tell me."**

`ON SCREEN: "👇 GOOD or SCARY?"`

---

### 📝 CAPTION

```
This cost $200/month yesterday. Today it's free.

Here's the breakdown:
→ [Company] moved [feature] into the free tier — no card, no waitlist
→ The service business built on top of it just lost its moat
→ The skill of using it well is now worth more than the access

Good news or scary news? I genuinely can't decide 👇

Source: [publication]

#AI #ArtificialIntelligence #AITools #TechNews #ContentCreator #CreativeAI #FutureOfWork #AIforCreators
```

---

### ✅ Pre-post checklist
- [ ] Opened the primary source myself — not just the newsletter summary
- [ ] Story is under 48 hours old
- [ ] One idea only
- [ ] Tool name spoken AND on screen
- [ ] Subtitles burned in (85% watch on mute)
- [ ] Hook format ≠ any hook I used in the last 7 days
- [ ] Posting between 8-10 PM IST
- [ ] Free for the first 30 min to reply to comments
